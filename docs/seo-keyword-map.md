## Internal linking model

The homepage remains the canonical commercial page. Future educational pages should link to the homepage with varied, descriptive anchors such as “Aura’s AI agent content-operation workflow”, “the agentic AI automation platform”, and “AI agents for content operations.” Supporting articles should cross-link to the relevant hub page and one adjacent topic where that connection genuinely helps the reader.

Do not publish the future URLs until each has substantial original explanatory content, clear ownership, and a reason to exist beyond targeting a query.
